Our Story

Amravati's Premier Unisex Salon & Spa

Founded in 1985, Roots Magma Unisex Salon (located at Next Level Mall, Ground Floor Shop No. 5, opposite Hotel Great Mehfil, Camp Road, Amravati) has been setting benchmarks in grooming and styling for over nearly four decades.

Under the visionary expertise of Praful Sir and our master stylists, we merge traditional hospitality with cutting-edge international techniques. From precision razor fades to transformative hair coloring and dermatological facials, every appointment is executed with meticulous detail.

  • Certified master stylists & skin therapists
  • Advanced Hydra-Facials, Keratin & Nanoplastia
  • Private, hygienic unisex styling & spa suites
